## Developer Platform

Over the past several years the company has grown from 40 to 310 engineers, and along the way each team picked its own CI system, its own deploy tooling, and its own observability stack. This was reasonable at the time. We now have six CI systems and four ways to deploy. Developer surveys show that engineers spend about 30 percent of their week on toolchain friction, and our two largest customers have both cited slow delivery of requested features. We looked at a number of options, including building a platform team, buying a vendor product, doing nothing, and asking each team to standardize on its own. After a lot of discussion, we have decided to fund a six-person platform team in Q1 that will own a single CI and deploy path, with the goal of moving every service onto it by the end of Q4. Teams will keep their own choice of language and framework.
