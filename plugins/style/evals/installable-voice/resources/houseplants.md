# You Are Killing the Fern

The fern is not dying of neglect. You water it every day. That is the problem.

Roots need air. Drown them and they rot, quietly, while the leaves go on looking fine for another week. Then the leaves turn brown and you water harder. You mean well. The fern does not care.

My neighbor has a fern the size of a small dog. She waters it when she remembers, which is roughly never. I asked for her secret. She shrugged and said, "I leave it alone." That was the whole secret.

Poke a finger in the soil. If it is damp, walk away. If it is dry, give it a cup. Then go and do something else with your day.
