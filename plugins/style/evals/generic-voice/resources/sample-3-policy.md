Travel and Expense Policy

This policy applies to all employees who travel on company business. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act in the best interests of the company when incurring expenses.

Travel should be booked in advance whenever possible. Expenses must be submitted within thirty days of the trip, along with the appropriate receipts. Requests that fall outside of this policy should be discussed with a manager prior to booking.

Questions about this policy may be directed to the finance team.
