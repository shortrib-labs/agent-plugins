# The Kettle Is Not Your Friend

You bought the kettle because the box said "rapid boil." It lies. Rapid is a feeling, not a number.

Here is what happens. You fill it to the line. You press the switch. You walk away to find a mug, and you come back to a kettle that has barely started. You blame the kettle. You should blame the box.

I once timed mine. Four minutes and ten seconds for a litre. My grandmother's stovetop pot did it in three, and nobody ever called that pot rapid. It just worked.

So stop trusting adjectives. Trust a stopwatch. Your tea will be fine either way, but at least you will know who to be angry at.
