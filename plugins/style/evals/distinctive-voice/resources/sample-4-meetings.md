# The Meeting Could Have Been a Sentence

You have been invited to a meeting titled "Sync." Nobody knows what it is syncing. You accept anyway, because declining feels rude.

Forty minutes later, someone says, "Let's take this offline." You nod. You both know offline is a place where things go to die.

Here is a trick. Before you accept, ask for the agenda. If there is none, the meeting is a rumor. Rumors do not need your calendar.

I tried this for a month. Eleven meetings vanished. Nobody missed them. One person thanked me, quietly, in the hallway, like we were trading state secrets.
