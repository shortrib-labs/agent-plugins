# On Parking Garages

Every parking garage is a small lesson in disappointment. You circle up. You circle up again. The spot you wanted is always one level higher than you are willing to go.

You know the move. You spot a gap, you signal, and a hatchback slides in from the other side like it has been waiting all day. You do not honk. Honking is for people who still believe in justice.

The ticket machine is worse. It wants exact change in a decade when nobody carries any. I keep a jar of quarters in the glovebox for this reason alone. My wife calls it paranoia. I call it a plan.

Park on the first level. Walk. Your knees will complain, but they complain anyway.
