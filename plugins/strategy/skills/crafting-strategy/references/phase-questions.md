# Phase Questions

Question banks for Phases 2 through 4. Read the section that matches where the conversation is.

## Refinement questions (Phase 2)

Use when something feels off:
- "You said X, but earlier you said Y - how do those fit together?"
- "Who would disagree with that assessment? What would they say?"
- "What data supports that? What would change your mind?"
- "Is that a fact or an assumption? How confident are you?"

## Larson's three policy questions (Phase 3)

1. "How should resources be allocated against this?"
2. "What rules must all teams follow?"
3. "How are decisions made - what can teams decide independently vs. what needs escalation?"

## Altitude check (Phase 3)

Each policy should pass:
- Is it **applicable** in real scenarios involving tradeoffs?
- Can it be **enforced** with clear accountability?
- Does it create **leverage** - compounding or multiplicative impact?

## Larson's action categories (Phase 4)

- **Enforcement:** How do we maintain policy compliance?
- **Escalation:** How do people constructively challenge the policy?
- **Transitions:** How do we move from current state to desired state?
