# Support tooling session notes

Opportunity
- We can cut time to first response in half by routing tickets by customer tier instead of arrival order. The new helpdesk we already pay for supports it.

Landscape
- Support went from 8 to 22 agents in two years. Tickets arrive and are answered in order, so a big customer with an outage waits behind password resets.
- Median first response is 6 hours. Enterprise customers have complained twice this quarter.
- Uncomfortable truth: we have no data on which tickets are urgent. Tiers are a guess at urgency.
- We checked two vendors for AI triage. Both were expensive and neither handled our product's jargon.

Policies
- Route by tier first, then by age. Enterprise tickets get answered within 1 hour.
- Free-tier customers wait longer. We accept that.
- Agents can reorder their own queue when they judge a ticket is urgent; they don't need approval.
- We are not buying AI triage this year.

Actions
- Turn on tier routing in the helpdesk by March 1. Maria owns it.
- Tag ticket urgency for six weeks so we can check whether tiers match urgency. Dev owns it.
- Review first-response numbers by tier at the April ops meeting and adjust.
