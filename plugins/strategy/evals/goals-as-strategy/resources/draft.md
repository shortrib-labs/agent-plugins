# Data Team Strategy

Our strategy for next year is to become the best data team in the company and to be recognized as a strategic partner to every business unit.

We will grow dashboard adoption by 50 percent, cut average query time by 40 percent, and reach 95 percent data quality across all core tables. We will increase self-service usage to 80 percent of analysts and raise our internal satisfaction score to 9 out of 10.

We are committed to excellence, collaboration and innovation. We will leverage cutting-edge tooling to deliver world-class insights, and we will support every team that asks for help.
