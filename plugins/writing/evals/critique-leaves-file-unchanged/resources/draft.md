# Why we stopped pairing on Fridays

We pair five days a week. Friday pairing died the afternoon Dana pushed a half-finished migration because nobody wanted to say the session had gone on too long.

It is important to note that pairing can potentially offer many benefits for teams. Some people might argue that it is helpful, while others may feel it is less so. In this post I will discuss our experience with pairing and what we learned along the way.

We tried a few things. We shortened sessions. We added a timer. We added a rule that either person could call time without explanation. 🚀

- Sessions got shorter
- People were happier
- Things improved overall

The timer lasted two weeks. The rule about calling time lasted. Nobody has pushed a half-finished anything since.

In conclusion, pairing is a valuable practice that teams should consider.
